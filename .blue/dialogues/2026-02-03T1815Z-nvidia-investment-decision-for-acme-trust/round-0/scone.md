[PERSPECTIVE P01: Correlation Risk Amplification]
Swapping NVAI for NVDA does not diversify AI exposure—it concentrates it further into the dominant player whose stock movements increasingly define the AI sector. When NVDA corrects, NVAI likely follows; the "diversification" benefit is illusory. True diversification would mean trimming both and allocating to non-AI sectors or cash.

[PERSPECTIVE P02: TSMC Single Point of Failure]
Both NVDA and the portfolio's broader tech holdings share TSMC fabrication dependency. A Taiwan Strait escalation creates correlated downside across NVDA, MSOFT, and likely NVAI—this geopolitical tail risk is under-weighted in the proposal.

[TENSION T01: Concentration vs. Conviction]
The proposal frames this as reducing single-stock risk, yet the portfolio already holds 14.4% cash and has energy/defense positions with lower ESG scores. Why prioritize AI rebalancing over deploying cash into underweight sectors or improving ESG profile?
